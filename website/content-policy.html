1. What this covers

This policy applies to any content users publish to the Sargam marketplace — tracks, titles, descriptions, and artwork ("User Content"). By publishing, you agree to these rules and to our Terms of Service.

2. Prohibited content

You may not upload content that:

  • infringes anyone's copyright, trademark, or other rights;
  • is illegal, or promotes illegal activity;
  • is hateful, harassing, threatening, or incites violence;
  • is sexually explicit or exploits minors;
  • is deceptive, spam, malware, or impersonates another person; or
  • contains samples or recordings you do not have the right to distribute.

3. Reporting content

If you see content that violates this policy, report it from within the app (the Report control on a track) or email subodhmishra765@gmail.com. You can also block a creator so you no longer see their content. We review reports and remove anything that violates this policy.

4. Copyright takedown

If you own rights to content published without permission, send a notice to subodhmishra765@gmail.com including:

  • identification of the work and the infringing track (a link);
  • your contact information;
  • a statement that you believe the use is unauthorized; and
  • a statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and you are the rights holder or authorized to act for them.

We aim to act on valid notices within [a few business days] and will notify the uploader.

5. Counter-notice

If your content was removed and you believe that was a mistake, you may submit a counter-notice to the same address explaining why you have the right to publish it. We may restore the content if appropriate.

6. Repeat infringers

We terminate the accounts of users who repeatedly infringe others' rights.

7. Our moderation

We may review, remove, or restrict any User Content that violates this policy or the Terms of Service, and may suspend accounts responsible for it. We are not obligated to host any particular content.
