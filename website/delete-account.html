Delete your account

Applies to the Sargam account you use to publish or buy tracks.

You can permanently delete your Sargam account and its associated data at any time. Choose whichever is easier:

Option 1 — In the app

  1. Open Settings Tap the gear icon (or, on mobile, the Settings tab).
  2. Choose "Delete account" It's in the Profile section, under your name.
  3. Confirm Your account and data are scheduled for deletion right away.

Option 2 — By email

Email subodhmishra765@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Sargam account". We'll verify it's you and process the deletion.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile — name and email.
  • Your published tracks and their marketplace listings.
  • Your wishlist and account settings.

This happens within 30 days of your request.

What we keep, and for how long

For legal, tax, and accounting reasons we retain records of completed transactions (purchases and payouts) for up to 7 years, after which they are deleted or anonymized. These records are kept only as required by law and are not used for any other purpose. Projects saved only on your own device are never uploaded to us, so they are yours to delete directly.
