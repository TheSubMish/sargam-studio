What you can create

A full studio, made for folk music

Everything you need to write, arrange, and export a piece — offline, with your work kept on your own device.

Multi-track arranging

Layer several instruments into one composition, each with its own volume, pan, and effects.

Taal templates

Start from real rhythmic cycles — Jhyaure, Tamang Selo, Teej — and build your groove from there.

Meend & gamak

Bend and ornament melodic lines the way folk instruments really move between notes.

Reverb & delay

Per-track plate reverb and feedback delay, plus a master fader — the mix is real DSP, not decoration.

Export anywhere

Bounce to WAV, export a MIDI file, or render per-track stems for games and further production.

Yours, on your device

Projects are plain files saved locally. Sargam runs offline — no account needed to compose.
