## Full description  (max 4000)

Sargam (सरगम) is a music studio for composing with Nepali folk instruments — right on your phone or tablet. Layer instruments, build rhythms from traditional taals, shape every note, and export your piece. It runs offline, and your work stays on your device.

🎵 REAL FOLK INSTRUMENTS
Tungna, damphu, madal, harmonium, bansuri, and sarangi — each built from physical-modeling synthesis, not canned loops. Plucked and bowed strings, struck drum heads, and breathy reeds that respond like the real thing.

🥁 START FROM A TAAL
Begin from authentic rhythmic cycles — Jhyaure, Tamang Selo, and Teej — then make them your own on the stroke grid. Adjust tempo and cycles to fit your piece.

🎼 COMPOSE YOUR WAY
• Multi-track arranging — a piano roll for melodies, a stroke grid for percussion
• Meend and gamak ornaments that bend and shape notes the way folk instruments really move
• Per-track volume, pan, reverb, and delay, plus a master level — real audio processing, not decoration
• A clean, warm interface designed around Nepali folk music

📤 EXPORT ANYWHERE
Bounce your composition to a WAV file, export standard MIDI, or render per-track stems — ready for game soundtracks, cultural projects, sync, or sharing with friends.

🔒 YOURS, AND OFFLINE
No account is needed to compose, and Sargam works without a connection. Your projects are ordinary files saved on your device — nothing is locked away.

🎧 DISCOVER
Browse the community marketplace and download free tracks to learn from, remix, or build on.

Whether you're scoring a game, preserving a tradition, or just making music for the joy of it, Sargam puts a Nepali folk studio in your pocket.
